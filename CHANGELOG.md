## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Switch Spaces without the slide animation: ⌃← and ⌃→ move one Space, ⌃⌘1 … ⌃⌘9 go to a desktop. Each group can be switched off in the menu.
- The menu bar shows the number of the current desktop.
- The menu warns when macOS's own ⌃← and ⌃→ shortcuts are on, since macOS then switches with the animation as well.
- Open at Login, through a login helper registered with macOS.
- Software updates through [Sparkle](https://sparkle-project.org). InstantSpaces checks for a new version once a week; Check for Updates in the menu checks at once, and "Check for Updates Automatically" turns the weekly check on or off.
